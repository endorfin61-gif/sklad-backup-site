# Privacy Policy

**Sklad Backup**

Last updated: October 10, 2026

## Overview

Sklad Backup is a private backup utility used by its owner to store encrypted backup copies of files in Google Drive. The application is not offered as a public service.

## Information Accessed

The application accesses Google Drive using authorization granted by the account owner. This access is used to create, read, and manage encrypted backup files.

## How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used solely for backup storage, verification, and restoration.

The application encrypts backup file contents and filenames before uploading them to Google Drive.

## Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally share Google user data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

## Storage and Security

Backup files are stored in the account owner's Google Drive in encrypted form.

Authorization credentials and encryption keys are managed by the account owner and must be kept secure.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the account owner or by configured backup retention procedures.

The account owner may revoke the application's Google Drive access through Google Account settings.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the application owner using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
